More than fifteen years in infrastructure, SRE and DevOps, from Linux hosting to leading a 25-person release engineering team at Deloitte Digital. Since 2022 I run CloudCat Digital and own the AWS and EKS platform behind an AI product for the construction industry: architecture, CI/CD, GPU capacity for ML workloads, security, observability and incident response.

Certified CKA, CKAD and CKS, HashiCorp Terraform and Vault Associate.

## How I work

- **Hands-on.** I work in your repos and your Slack, open pull requests, and take the pager for what I build until your team is comfortable with it.
- **Written down.** Decisions, runbooks and findings end up in your wiki and your repositories, not in my head.
- **Boring on purpose.** Fewer moving parts, rehearsed upgrades, and alerts that only fire when someone needs to act.
